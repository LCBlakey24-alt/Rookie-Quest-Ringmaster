# Loading Intro Video Storyboard (30 seconds)

## Goal
Create a short cinematic loading intro that sells the fantasy: **you are not a wrestler, you are the mastermind behind the company**.

## Sequence
1. **0s–4s**: Black screen, arena crowd rumble fades in, logo appears.
2. **4s–8s**: Text: "Every decision writes history." over abstract lights.
3. **8s–14s**: Fast cuts of booking board, contract sheet, injury alert, title belt silhouette.
4. **14s–20s**: Text: "Build stars. Survive chaos. Own the business."
5. **20s–26s**: Pulse flare + logo lockup + tagline.
6. **26s–30s**: "Loading Universe..." + subtle moving background loop.

## Audio
- Deep crowd ambience bed
- Low cinematic braam at 14s
- Soft heartbeat kick under final loading segment

## Delivery Specs
- Master: 1920x1080, 30fps, H.264 MP4
- Optional console variants: 4K and 720p
- Loudness target (mix pass later): -16 LUFS integrated
