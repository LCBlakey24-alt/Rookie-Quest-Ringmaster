# Game Modes (Playstyle Framework)

## 1) Promoter Mode (Full Control)
You run the entire company:
- set budgets and expansion
- choose shows/PPVs and cadence
- control stories and winners
- manage roster contracts and long-term direction

Best for players who want maximum strategy depth and full narrative control.

## 2) General Manager Mode (Show-Centric)
You run a show, not the whole company:
- book matches and pacing
- optimize card quality and ratings race
- **you do not decide winners** (outcomes are simulation/creative-team driven)
- limited control over finances and long-term company policy

Best for streamers/commentators and players who want emergent results.

## 3) Wrestler Career Mode (Single-Talent RPG-lite)
You play as one wrestler:
- train skills (promo, finisher, safety, stamina)
- build popularity and locker-room reputation
- negotiate contracts and manage career choices
- try to become champion/main-event draw over seasons

Best for role-playing progression and personal storytelling.

---

## Shared Goal
All modes connect to the same world simulation so outcomes in one mode can influence others over time.
